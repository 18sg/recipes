Sticky Belly Pork for 4
=======================

Quick and easy. Serve with rice.

```recipe
500g pork belly, slice 2cm cubes
4tbsp honey
4tbsp soy sauce
4 garlic cloves, mince
2 tsp chilli flakes

simmer to desired consistency(
    coat(
        fry until brown and rind crisps(
            pork belly
        ),
        honey,
        soy sauce,
        garlic cloves,
        chilli flakes
    )
)
```
