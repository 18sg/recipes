Liver and Bacon for 4
=====================

Lovely liver and bacon recipe with a rich sauce. Works with calves or lambs liver.

```recipe
450g liver, cut into 1cm slices
4tsp plain flour
20g butter
1tsp sunflower oil
1 medium onion, finely slice
2 rashers bacon, cut into 2cm slices
500ml beef stock
2tsp tomato ketchup
salt
pepper


pan, browned liver = fry medium heat until lightly browned(
  coat(
    rinse and pat dry(
      liver
    ),
    mix in a bowl(
      1/2 of the plain flour,
      salt,
      pepper
    )
  ),
  sunflower oil,
  1/2 of the butter
)

heat 3mins(
  simmer to nice consistency(
    stir in(
      slowly stir in(
        coat(
          fry until bacon browned(
            fry medium-low heat until lightly browned(
              medium onion,
              remaining butter,
              pan
            ),
            rashers bacon
          ),
          remaining plain flour
        ),
        beef stock
      ),
      tomato ketchup
    )
  ),
  browned liver
)
```
