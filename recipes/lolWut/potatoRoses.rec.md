Potato Roses for 6
==================

Rediculous but extremely tasty side. If they start to over-brown before cooked through, lightly gover with foil.

    1.4kg potatoes
    75g butter, melt
    1tbsp rosemary
    2tsp salt
    0.75tsp black pepper
    0.5tsp cayenne pepper
    80g parmigiano-reggiano, grated

    bake until brown and cooked through(
        preheat oven 200c,
        arrange slices working up edges of tin into a rose shape finishing with small pieces in middle(
            grease muffin tin with some of the butter,
            toss in a litte at a time(
                toss(
                    discard end pieces that are mainly skin(
                        slice approx 1mm on mandoline(
                            potatoes)),
                    butter,
                    rosemary,
                    salt,
                    black pepper,
                    cayenne pepper),
                parmigiano-reggiano)))
