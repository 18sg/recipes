Cornbread
=========

A nice and easy recipe for American-style cornbread. It's a little dry, sweet, and almost cake like. It's normally eaten with American-style BBQ and its associated sauces. [Original source](https://eatlittlebird.com/cornbread-recipe/).

	175g fine cornmeal
	125g plain flour
	45g sugar
	0.5tsp salt
	1tbsp baking powder
	250ml milk
	1 egg
	50g butter


	rest 5-10mins(
		bake 20-25mins until golden brown(
			preheat oven 180degC,
			pour in and level(
				grease 26cm cast iron skillet with extra butter,
				mix(
					lightly mix until just combined(
						mix(fine cornmeal, plain flour, sugar, salt, baking powder),
						mix(milk, egg)),
					melt(butter)))))
