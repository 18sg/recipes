Hot Hamburger Sandwich for 2
============================

Traditional Canadian comfort food. It's a burger on top of a slice of bread covered in gravy. Much nicer than it deserves to be! Serve with garden peas and chips.

    500g minced beef
    1tbsp worcestershire sauce
    1tbsp garlic powder
    1tbsp onion powder
    1 egg
    2 medium onions, dice
    4cups mushrooms, slice
    2tbsp butter
    2tbsp flour
    375ml beef broth
    1tbsp marmite
    4 slices of white bread

    burgers := remove to plate(
        brown(
            form into {4} patties(
                mix(
                    minced beef,
                    worcestershire sauce,
                    garlic powder,
                    salt,
                    pepper,
                    onion powder,
                    egg)),
            oil))

    top(
        cover and simmer until cooked through(
            return burgers to pan(
                season(
                    stir in(
                        stir in(
                            melt(
                                fry until mushrooms golden(
                                    fry in burger pan until golden(
                                        medium onions,
                                        oil),
                                    mushrooms),
                                butter),
                            flour),
                        beef broth,
                        marmite)),
                burgers)),
        slices of white bread)
