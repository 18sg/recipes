Creamed Corn for 8
==================

A tasty recipe for American-style creamed corn. Normally eaten with American-style BBQ. If you're British, there's enough sugar in this that its essentially desert. [Original source](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/53304/cream-corn-like-no-other/).

	550g frozen corn
	240ml double cream
	1tsp salt
	2tbsp sugar
	0.25tsp black pepper
	2tbsp butter
	120ml milk
	2tbsp plain flour
	60ml parmesan, grate

	stir through(
		remove from head(
			cook approx 4mins until thickened(
				cook approx 5mins over medium heat until butter melted(frozen corn, double cream, salt, sugar, black pepper, butter),
				whisk together(milk, plain flour))),
		parmesan)
