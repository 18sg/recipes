Purée de Tomate for 4
=====================

This historical tomato soup pre-dates vegetarianism and therefor contains ham. It's also supposed to be fancy, so contains shallots and is named in french. Smoked ham is preferred. Add more ham and cream to taste. Generous portions. Adapted from [this Glen and Friend's video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ixnMDHfkAGM).

	60g Ham, dice
	1 Onion, dice
	0.5 Carrot, dice
	1 Stick Celery, dice
	1 Shallot, dice
	30g Butter
	1tbsp Vinegar
	Salt
	Pepper
	Nutmeg
	3 cans Tomatoes
	1.7l Stock
	30g Cornflour
	70ml Cream

	mix(
		boil 5 mins(
			blend(
				simmer 15-20mins (
					sauté 5 mins (Ham, Onion, Carrot, Stick Celery, Shallot, Butter, Vinegar, Salt, Pepper, Nutmeg),
					Tomatoes)),
			mix(Stock, Cornflour)),
		Cream)
