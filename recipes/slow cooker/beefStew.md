Beef Stew for 6
===============

True zero-pre-cooking slow cooker beef stew. Really tasty and very easy. Serve
with mash.

    mix up in slow cooker and cook for 8-10 hours on low (
        600ml boiling water,
        crumble(1 beef stock cube),
        2 tbsp tomato purée,
        1 tbsp Marmite,
        cut into 3cm chunks(900g braising steak),
        100g smoked bacon lardons,
        thinly slice(2 onions),
        cut into 3cm chunks(peel(4 carrots)),
        cut into 3cm lengths(4 celery sticks),
        3 tbsp plain flour,
        1 tsp salt,
        1 tsp dried mixed herbs,
        1 bay leaf,
        ground black pepper,
    )
